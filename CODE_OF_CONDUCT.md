# Code of Conduct

BombayChitra follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, patient and respectful; assume good intent; give and accept constructive feedback; and
focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, insults and personal or political attacks are not acceptable
in issues, pull requests or any other project space.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through a
[GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/AmmaarBakshi/BombayChitra/security/advisories/new) or by contacting a
maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially, and maintainers may remove content or block participants
who do not follow this code.
